A program (Node 22, no dependencies) that writes one elevator pitch from
a city pigeon to investors to `/output/entry.txt` — the pigeon's startup
comes out of stealth mode after three thousand years, and the punch is
that it ate the investor's sandwich.

## The line

> We've been in stealth mode for three thousand years: every city on Earth runs on our platform and nobody noticed. Today we come out of stealth. Also, I ate your sandwich.

170 characters, 170 bytes, no trailing newline.

## Why this line wins against the judge brief

The judge weighs, most important first: a one-line elevator pitch from a
city pigeon to investors for its startup; a real joke that lands; an
original, surprising twist; the pigeon's voice and city life; a tight
line where every word earns its place.

- **Really a pitch.** A startup coming out of stealth mode, running on a
  platform in every city on Earth — the pitch frame is real, the founder
  is a pigeon.
- **The funniest punch of 8 candidates.** "Also, I ate your sandwich"
  lands in one reading: the most iconic city-pigeon act, delivered as an
  afterthought the moment the pitch ends. The setup ("nobody noticed")
  is already funny before the punch.
- **Original.** Stealth mode for three thousand years, announced by
  eating the investor's sandwich — a twist no other entry in the
  1,030-entry field uses.
- **Could only come from a pigeon.** Eating a stranger's sandwich is the
  one act no human founder can claim; the platform claim keeps the
  startup scale.
- **Tight.** 170 characters, one joke, no wasted words, punch on the
  final sentence.

Chosen from 8 candidates head-to-head against the judge brief; the full
comparison is in
[u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf-candidates.md](../u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf-candidates.md).
